01 · Custom Kitchen · Bay Area, CA

White inset cabinetry with full-height refrigerator wall

White inset cabinetry built around a full-height stainless side-by-side refrigerator. Marble subway backsplash. Integrated trash and recycling pullouts.

Inset doors with classical raised-panel detail. Brushed nickel hardware. Quartz countertops with eased edge.

A six-week build that turns an awkward fridge wall into the cleanest run in the room.

Materials & details

  • White painted hardwood
  • Marble subway backsplash
  • Inset raised-panel doors
  • Quartz counters
  • Brushed nickel hardware
  • Integrated pullouts
