03 — Our Process

From first meeting to final install.

Four steps. Same shop end to end.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We meet at your place or our Citrus Heights showroom.

  2. 02

    Design

    Measured drawings, material samples, written proposal.

  3. 03

    Build

    Six to ten weeks in the workshop, with weekly photos.

  4. 04

    Install

    One to two weeks on site. Punch list cleared at end.

See materials and process detail for each service line +
01 Custom Kitchens

Built around the way your family lives.

Complete kitchen cabinetry for new builds and full remodels. Perimeter runs, islands, pantry walls, integrated appliances — drawn, built, and installed by the workshop.

  • Plain-sawn white oak · Rift walnut · Painted maple
  • Hardwood plywood carcasses · soft-close hardware throughout
  • Conversion-varnish or oil-rubbed finishes
02 Bathroom Cabinetry

Bathroom cabinetry, built for the long term.

Vanities, linen towers, and medicine cabinets built around your bathroom. Marine-grade carcasses and conversion-varnish finishes that hold up for decades.

  • Single or dual vanity · linen tower · medicine cabinet
  • Marine-grade plywood carcass · conversion-varnish finishes
  • Design 2–4 weeks · build 6–10 weeks
03 Paneled Rooms + Millwork

Detail that makes a room feel built.

Coffered ceilings, raised wainscoting, fluted columns, library shelving, door and window casings. We work directly with architects and designers.

  • Walnut · White oak · Mahogany · Painted poplar
  • Oil-rubbed finishes · period-profile match capability
04 Built-Ins + Specialty

Bookshelves, window seats, bar cabinets, bespoke storage.

Built-ins have to land within a millimeter. Refrigerator surrounds, mudroom benches, window-seat banquettes, fireplace surrounds, entry storage.

  • Single unit or full wall · live-edge & reclaimed wood
  • Custom wine storage · bookshelves · window seats · bar cabinets
  • 3–6 week lead time
05 Restoration

Refinishing, refacing, and restoration of period cabinetry.

Sometimes the answer is not new cabinetry. Older kitchens and built-ins were often made from solid wood with joinery worth saving. We restore them to last another generation.

  • Door refinishing · hardware retrofit · new drawer fronts
  • Interior storage upgrades · on-site assessment before quoting
  • 2–6 weeks typical timeline
