Portfolio · Eight selected projects

Projects

A selection of recent work.

Kitchens, baths, paneled rooms, built-ins, and specialty millwork from the Citrus Heights workshop. Eight projects, presented at length. Locations and completion years carry "TBC" placeholders until confirmed with each homeowner.

Kitchen · Bay Area · Year TBC

Plain-sawn walnut and matte black.

A full kitchen built around a single piece of honed stone. Matte-black perimeter cabinetry in painted MDF, walnut island in plain-sawn cuts taken from one log to keep the grain reading consistent.

European concealed hinges, full-extension soft-close runners, paneled refrigerator surround, integrated dishwasher. The hood is custom millwork in walnut to match the island front.

Paneled Room · Sacramento · Year TBC

Walnut game room with a coffered ceiling.

Floor-to-ceiling walnut paneling with raised panel insets, a coffered ceiling laid out to the room's geometry, fluted columns at the openings, and an alcove sized to the billiards table.

Two months of shop time. Two weeks of install. Built and finished entirely in Citrus Heights, dry-fit on the floor before it traveled.

Kitchen · Sacramento · Year TBC

Arched glass and a slab-marble island.

Floor-to-ceiling arched glass display cabinets framing a slab-marble island. One of the most distinctive cabinet runs in the portfolio.

The arches were drawn at full scale on the shop floor before any glass was cut. Painted maple frames, hand-rubbed lacquer finish, museum-clear glass set with brass beading.

Bathroom · Sacramento · Year TBC

Hollywood Regency dual vanity.

A primary bath built around a pair of arched mirrors, sconce-lit fluted columns, and a stone counter that returns into the wall on both ends. Marine-grade plywood carcass, solid wood face frames, conversion-varnish finish.

Drawer banks run on integrated outlet trays. Linen tower to the right of the vanity carries the same column detail.

Built-In · Year TBC

Integrated refrigerator surround.

A panel-ready refrigerator built into a wall of veined slab marble. Concealed-panel doors flank the unit. The whole assembly reads as a continuous architectural element rather than an appliance.

Site tolerance was the hardest part. The framing was out by a quarter inch; the cabinetry was scribed to it on install day.

Kitchen · Sacramento · Year TBC

Transitional white and a waterfall island.

A transitional kitchen with painted shaker cabinetry, French-window display niches above the perimeter, and a waterfall marble island. Wrought-iron pendants over the island. Hardwood floors.

Pantry wall conceals a coffee station behind tambour doors. Range alcove built into the run as a single architectural moment.

Specialty Millwork · Year TBC

Live-edge walnut, reclaimed bench.

A one-off commission. Live-edge walnut floating shelves hung from concealed steel brackets, paired with a reclaimed-wood bench fabricated from old-growth fir.

The walnut was sourced as a single slab and resawn in the shop. Bookmatched joinery on the bench. Hand-rubbed oil finish on both pieces.

Mudroom + Pantry · Year TBC

Mudroom built into the bones of the house.

A back-of-house cabinetry wall doing the work of three separate rooms: drop zone, pantry, laundry surround. Cream-painted shaker fronts on a full-height storage run, integrated bench seating with hidden boot storage below.

Doors hang on solid brass butt hinges. Drawer fronts run on full-extension soft-close. Counter is solid maple, oiled, sanded back twice a year by the owner.
