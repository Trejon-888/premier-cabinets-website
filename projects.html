Plain-sawn white oak kitchen for Bay Area residence.

  • Private client
  • San Francisco Bay Area
  • Year TBC

A full kitchen built around a single piece of honed stone. Perimeter cabinetry in plain-sawn white oak, island and pantry run finished in painted hardwood with hand-rubbed oil on the show faces.

European concealed hinges throughout. Full-extension soft-close runners. Paneled refrigerator surround, integrated dishwasher, custom range hood in matching white oak. Designed for a family that cooks daily.

Drawn, built, and installed by the workshop. Six weeks in shop, one week on site.
