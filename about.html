About

Four decades of cabinet craft.

A custom cabinetry and millwork workshop in Citrus Heights, California. Founded in 1985 by Felix. Still in the same town. Still the same owner.

01 — The Workshop

A small shop. Custom work only.

Premier Cabinets Innovations is a custom cabinetry and architectural millwork workshop. We build kitchens, baths, paneled rooms, and built-ins for homes across Sacramento and the Bay Area.

The workshop is small on purpose. We take on a limited number of projects each year and run each one from the same room: design, build, finish, and install. We do not sub out the cabinets or the final fit.

02 — Felix

The owner.

Felix founded Premier Cabinets Innovations in 1985 and personally oversees every project from the Citrus Heights workshop.

Founder portrait and full team profile coming with the next site update.

03 — Service Area

Sacramento. Bay Area. Northern California.

The workshop sits in Citrus Heights, twenty minutes north of downtown Sacramento. We work routinely across the Sacramento metro and the San Francisco Bay Area, with destination projects throughout Northern California.
