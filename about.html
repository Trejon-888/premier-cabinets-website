01 — The Workshop

A workshop ethic, since 1985.

Premier Cabinets Innovations has built custom kitchens, bathroom cabinetry, and architectural millwork from a single shop in Citrus Heights since 1985. The work is measured, drawn, cut, joined, finished, and installed by the same small team.

Felix founded the workshop in 1985 and personally oversees every project. Forty-one years on, the phone still rings to the same place.
