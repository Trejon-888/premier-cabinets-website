About the workshop

Meet Felix. Forty-one years of cabinet craft.

When you call Premier Cabinets Innovations, you talk to Felix. He's been drawing, building, and installing cabinets from the same Citrus Heights workshop since 1985.

Felix opened the shop in 1985. The phone has been at the same address ever since. Forty-one years on, the work passes through fewer hands than at any other cabinet shop in the region.

Every project is measured, drawn, cut, joined, finished, and installed by the same small team. Felix is on every consultation, every drawing review, and every final walkthrough.

We work with homeowners directly and with the architects and designers they trust. Most of our work comes from referrals — people who had Felix do their kitchen telling their friends he'll do theirs too.
