The Story

Older than the modern shops. Younger in how we work.

Premier Cabinets Innovations opened in 1985 in Citrus Heights, California. Forty-one years later it is still the same shop, still in the same town, still building cabinets and millwork by hand. The address has not moved. The phone number has not changed. The owner is the same person who started it.

Felix runs the workshop. He sets the cut list on every project, draws the joinery details, supervises the finishing room, and shows up for every install. The same person who answers the phone on Monday is the person assembling your face frames on Wednesday. That is the shape of the business and it is on purpose.

The work has changed. In 1985 most of the portfolio was traditional kitchens for Sacramento-area homes. Over four decades the shop has built almost every house style you can name. Tuscan and Italianate in the late 1990s. Hollywood Regency through the 2000s. Modern matte black and white shaker through the 2010s. The portfolio today crosses contemporary, transitional, and traditional work, with paneled rooms and architectural millwork sitting alongside the cabinetry that started the shop.

We build for homeowners, designers, and contractors across Sacramento and the Bay Area. Most of our work comes from referrals.

— Felix

The customer base has been built by referral, not advertising. The work has traveled from Citrus Heights into the rest of the Sacramento metro and across into the Bay Area, where designers and homeowners working on larger projects have brought the shop in for kitchens, paneled rooms, and full millwork packages.

The size of the workshop is the constraint and the feature. We take on a limited number of projects each year. That limit is what lets us run every project from the same room: design here, build here, finish here, install with our own crew. We do not sub out the cabinets. We do not sub out the millwork. We do not sub out the final fit.

If the work in the portfolio matches the room you have in mind, the next step is a phone call. We will tell you whether we are the right shop for it.

Service Area

Sacramento metro, Bay Area, and the towns in between.

The workshop sits in Citrus Heights, twenty minutes north of downtown Sacramento. We work routinely across the Sacramento metro: Sacramento proper, Roseville, Rocklin, Folsom, Granite Bay, El Dorado Hills, Fair Oaks, Carmichael, and points east into Auburn and Placerville.

A significant share of our work happens in the San Francisco Bay Area. We have completed kitchens, paneled rooms, and full millwork packages in San Francisco, Oakland, Berkeley, Walnut Creek, and the Peninsula. Most Bay Area work comes to us through architects and designers we have built relationships with over the years.

If your project sits between those two metros or somewhere on either edge, ask. We have likely worked nearby.

The Team
