What to expect

Three steps, no pressure.

Step One

The call.

Thirty minutes. We listen first. What you're picturing, what's prompting the project, what your timeline looks like. We share what we typically build at the price you're considering and whether the fit feels right on both sides.

Step Two

The walk.

If the call surfaces a real project, we set up an in-person walk. Your place or our Citrus Heights workshop. We measure, look at materials, and put the first sketch on paper together.

Step Three

The drawing.

A scaled drawing with materials, finishes, and a written estimate. From there, you decide. We don't push. Most of our clients have lived with their drawing for a few weeks before signing.
