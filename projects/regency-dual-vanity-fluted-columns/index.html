02 · Bathroom Vanity · Bay Area, CA

Hollywood Regency dual vanity with fluted columns

Master bath dual vanity built in white painted hardwood with fluted column detailing between the two basin runs. Arched mirror cabinets above each basin.

Custom fluted columns turned and assembled in-shop. Inset doors and drawers throughout. Soft-close hardware. Marble countertop with integrated undermount basins.

Eight weeks shop, three days on site. A symmetric room from end to end.

Materials & details

  • Painted hardwood
  • Turned fluted columns
  • Marble counter
  • Arched mirror cabinets
  • Soft-close inset hardware
  • Undermount basins
