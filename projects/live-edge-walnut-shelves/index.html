Live-edge walnut shelves and reclaimed-fir bench

Two live-edge walnut floating shelves over a reclaimed-fir built-in bench. Designed for a transitional entry-mudroom moment.

Walnut slabs hand-selected for matching edge profile. Oil-rubbed finish. The bench seat is solid reclaimed Douglas fir over a painted-hardwood base with shoe storage below.

Four-week build. The piece reads as part of the architecture, not as added furniture.

Materials & details

  • Live-edge walnut slabs
  • Reclaimed Douglas fir
  • Painted hardwood base
  • Oil-rubbed finish
  • Hidden shelf brackets
  • Shoe storage below
