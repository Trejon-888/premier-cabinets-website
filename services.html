01 Custom Kitchens

Built around the way your family lives.

We design and build complete kitchen cabinetry packages for new construction and full remodels. Perimeter runs, islands, pantry walls, integrated appliances. Drawn, built, and installed by the workshop.

Materials we work in regularly
  • Plain-sawn white oak
  • Rift walnut
  • Painted maple
  • Hardwood plywood carcasses
  • Soft-close hardware throughout
  • Conversion-varnish or hand-rubbed oil finishes

02 Bathroom Cabinetry

Bathroom cabinetry, built for the long term.

Vanities, linen towers, medicine cabinets, and storage built around your bathroom layout. Marine-grade carcasses, sealed end-grain, and conversion-varnish finishes that hold up for decades.

Typical scope
  • Single or dual vanity
  • Linen tower & medicine cabinet
  • Marine-grade plywood carcass
  • Conversion-varnish finishes
  • Design 2–4 weeks
  • Build 6–10 weeks

04 Built-Ins + Specialty

Bookshelves, window seats, bar cabinets, and bespoke storage.

Built-ins are small projects with the highest tolerance for error. They have to land within a millimeter. Refrigerator surrounds, mudroom benches, window-seat banquettes, fireplace surrounds, entry storage, wine cellars.

Typical scope
  • Single unit or full wall
  • Live-edge & reclaimed wood
  • Custom wine storage
  • Bookshelves & window seats
  • Bar cabinets
  • 3–6 week lead time
Discuss a built-in →
05 Restoration

Refinishing, refacing, and restoration of period cabinetry.

Sometimes the right answer is not new cabinetry. Older kitchens, libraries, and built-ins were often built from solid wood with joinery that holds up better than a mid-market replacement would. We restore them to last another generation.

Typical scope
  • Door refinishing
  • Hardware retrofit
  • New drawer fronts
  • Interior storage upgrades
  • On-site assessment before quoting
  • 2–6 weeks typical timeline

Our Process

From first meeting to final install.

Four steps. Same shop end to end. We don't sub out the design, the cabinets, or the install.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We meet at your home or our Citrus Heights showroom to understand your space, materials, and goals.

  2. 02

    Design

    Measured drawings, material samples, and a written proposal. Approval before any sawdust.

  3. 03

    Build

    Six to ten weeks in the workshop. You receive weekly photo updates.

  4. 04

    Install

    One to two weeks on site. Punch list cleared before final payment.
