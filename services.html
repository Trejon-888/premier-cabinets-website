01 Custom Kitchens

The room the house gets built around.

Custom kitchens are the deepest part of the practice. We design and build complete cabinetry packages for new construction and full remodels: perimeter runs, islands, pantry walls, integrated appliances.

We work across styles. Recent kitchens have ranged from matte black with walnut accents to white shaker, transitional marble-and-brass, and arched-glass formal. We do not push a house style on you.

  • 20 to 60 linear feet typical scope
  • White oak, walnut, maple, painted MDF
  • Design 3 to 6 weeks, build 8 to 12 weeks
  • Install 5 to 10 days
Discuss your kitchen →
02 Bathroom Cabinetry

Built for moisture, finished for the long term.

Bathrooms are the cabinet shop's hardest finish problem. We build bath cabinetry on marine-grade plywood carcasses with sealed end-grain and conversion-varnish finishes that hold up for decades.

The portfolio runs from Hollywood Regency dual vanities to modern floating vanities with vessel sinks, to clean white shaker on stone counters.

  • Single or dual vanity, linen tower, medicine cabinet
  • Marine-grade plywood carcass
  • Conversion-varnish finishes
  • Design 2 to 4 weeks, build 6 to 10 weeks
Discuss your bath →
03 Paneled Rooms + Millwork

Detail that makes a room feel built.

Architectural millwork is the work most cabinet shops do not take on. Coffered ceilings, raised wainscoting, fluted columns, library shelving, paneled dens and game rooms, door and window casings.

We work directly with architects and interior designers on this scope. We can match historical profiles or carry a contemporary library detail through an entire floor.

  • Single feature room through full-house trim packages
  • Walnut, white oak, mahogany, painted poplar
  • One of the few shops that builds cabinets and millwork under one roof
  • Direct collaboration with architect and GC

04 Built-Ins + Specialty Work

If it's wood and it has to fit perfectly.

Built-ins are the small projects with the highest tolerance for error. Refrigerator surrounds, mudroom benches, window-seat banquettes, fireplace surrounds, entry storage. They have to land within a millimeter.

Specialty work covers everything outside the standard lines. Live-edge floating shelves. Reclaimed-wood benches. One-off display cases. Wine storage.

  • Single unit through full mudroom walls
  • Live-edge, reclaimed wood, custom wine storage
  • Standalone or paired with larger projects
  • 3 to 6 week lead time on smaller scope
Discuss a built-in →
05 Restoration + Refinishing

When the cabinets are worth saving.

Sometimes the right answer is not new cabinetry. Older kitchens, libraries, and built-ins were often built from solid wood with joinery that holds up better than a mid-market replacement would.

Scope ranges from rehanging doors with new soft-close hardware and a fresh varnish topcoat, through complete refinishing with new doors and drawer fronts.

  • Door refinishing, hardware retrofit
  • New drawer fronts, interior storage upgrades
  • On-site assessment before quoting
  • 2 to 6 weeks typical timeline
