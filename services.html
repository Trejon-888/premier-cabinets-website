Five service lines

What We Build

Custom cabinetry + architectural millwork.

Five lines of work, one shop. Felix and the workshop run every project end-to-end. We design the cabinets, build them in Citrus Heights, and install them ourselves.

01 Custom Kitchens

The room the house gets built around.

Custom kitchens are the deepest part of the practice. We design and build complete cabinetry packages for new construction and full remodels: perimeter runs, islands, pantry walls, integrated appliances, and the interior fittings most shops sub out.

Every kitchen is drawn from a measured site visit. We work in plain-sawn white oak, rift walnut, painted maple, lacquered MDF, and reclaimed species when the project calls for it. Hardware is specified by job. We carry European concealed hinges, soft-close runners, and the integration kits needed for paneled refrigerators and dishwashers.

We work across styles. Recent kitchens have ranged from contemporary matte black with walnut accents to white shaker, transitional marble-and-brass, and arched-glass formal. We do not push a house style on you; we build what the room wants.

Typical scope

20 to 60 linear feet of cabinetry, island, integrated appliance package, full hardware spec.

Materials

White oak, walnut, maple, painted MDF, reclaimed species on request. Slab marble, quartz, soapstone counters coordinated.

Timeline

Design 3 to 6 weeks. Build 8 to 12 weeks. Install 5 to 10 days.

Service area

Sacramento metro and the San Francisco Bay Area.

02 Bathroom Cabinetry

Built for moisture, finished for the long term.

Bathrooms are the cabinet shop's hardest finish problem. Vanities live with daily standing water, steam, and a humidity cycle no kitchen sees. We build bath cabinetry on marine-grade plywood carcasses, with sealed end-grain and conversion-varnish finishes that hold up for decades, not seasons.

The portfolio runs from Hollywood Regency dual vanities with arched mirrors and fluted columns, through modern floating vanities with vessel sinks and beveled mirrors, to clean white shaker vanities with blackened hardware on stone counters.

Standard scope includes the vanity body, drawer banks, integrated outlet trays, linen towers, medicine cabinets, and wall-mount storage. We coordinate countertop fabricators, hardware, and lighting on the same drawing set.

Typical scope

Single or dual vanity, linen tower, medicine cabinet, integrated outlet trays.

Materials

Marine-grade plywood, conversion-varnish finishes, solid wood face frames and doors.

Timeline

Design 2 to 4 weeks. Build 6 to 10 weeks. Install 2 to 5 days.

Pairs well with

Bath remodels, primary suite additions, ADU and guest-suite builds.

03 Paneled Rooms + Millwork

The detail that makes a room feel built, not assembled.

Architectural millwork is the work most cabinet shops do not take on. We do. Coffered ceilings, raised wainscoting, fluted columns, library shelving, paneled dens and game rooms, door and window casings, raised hearths, and the decorative trim that ties an interior to its architecture.

The paneled game room in the portfolio is a representative project: floor-to-ceiling walnut paneling, a coffered ceiling laid out to the room's geometry, fluted columns at the openings, an alcove for a billiards table, and integrated cabinetry for glassware and equipment. Two months of shop time, two weeks of install.

We work directly with architects and interior designers on this scope. We can match historical profiles, draw new ones, or carry a contemporary library detail through an entire floor of a house.

Typical scope

Single feature room (library, den, game room) through full-house trim packages.

Materials

Walnut, white oak, mahogany, painted poplar, MDF carcass with solid wood profiles.

Coordination

Direct collaboration with architect, GC, and interior designer on shop drawings.

Differentiator

One of the few shops in Sacramento that builds the cabinets and the millwork under one roof.

04 Built-Ins + Specialty Work

If it's wood and it has to fit perfectly.

Built-ins are the small projects with the highest tolerance for error. Refrigerator surrounds, integrated appliance panels, mudroom benches, pantry walls, window-seat banquettes, fireplace surrounds, closet systems, and entry storage. They have to land within a millimeter of the surrounding architecture.

Specialty work is everything that does not fit one of the four standard lines. Live-edge floating shelves in walnut. Reclaimed-wood benches. One-off display cases. Wine storage. We have built each of these inside larger projects and as standalone commissions.

If you have a room with an awkward wall, a too-deep alcove, or a fixed appliance that needs to be hidden, this is the service line. Pricing scales with complexity, not square footage.

Typical scope

Single built-in unit through full mudroom or pantry walls.

Specialty examples

Live-edge shelving, reclaimed-wood benches, custom wine storage, banquettes.

Standalone or paired

Built-ins can run as their own project or fold into a larger kitchen or remodel scope.

Lead time

Smaller built-ins often slot into existing shop schedule with 3 to 6 weeks lead.

05 Restoration + Refinishing

When the cabinets are worth saving.

Sometimes the right answer is not new cabinetry. Older kitchens, libraries, and built-ins were often built from solid wood with joinery that holds up better than what a mid-market replacement would deliver. We assess existing cabinetry on its bones and either restore the original work, refit the interiors with modern hardware, or refinish surfaces to bring the wood back.

Restoration scope can range from rehanging doors with new soft-close hardware and a fresh conversion-varnish topcoat, through complete refinishing with new doors, drawer fronts, and interior storage. We can match original profiles when wholesale replacement of one section is needed.

Restoration is a smaller share of our work but a meaningful one. It is also the right starting point if you are unsure whether to remodel or replace.

Common scope

Door refinishing, hardware retrofit, new drawer fronts, interior storage upgrades.

Assessment

On-site inspection before quoting. We will tell you honestly whether restoration makes sense.

Match work

We can match historical door profiles and stain colors on partial replacements.

Timeline

2 to 6 weeks for typical refinishing scope.
