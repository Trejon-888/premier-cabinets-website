03 · Wall Paneling · Sacramento, CA

Walnut library and study paneling

Floor-to-ceiling walnut paneling with coffered ceiling detail. Inset bookcases on the long wall, full-height bar millwork at the back.

Hand-selected book-matched walnut veneer on quarter-sawn substrate. Hand-rubbed oil finish. Solid walnut crown and base moldings cut and joined in-shop.

A ten-week build from measured drawings to final install. The room reads as one continuous piece of joinery.

Materials & details

  • Quarter-sawn walnut veneer
  • Solid walnut moldings
  • Oil-rubbed finish
  • Coffered ceiling millwork
  • Inset bookcases
  • Full-height bar cabinetry
