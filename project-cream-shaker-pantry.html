05 · Built-In Storage · Sacramento, CA

Cream shaker walk-in pantry with full-height storage

Walk-in pantry built entirely in painted cream shaker. Full-height cabinetry from baseboard to ceiling, with adjustable interior shelving and pullout drawer banks.

Inset shaker doors. Soft-close hardware. Hand-applied finish in a warm cream specified to match the adjacent kitchen palette.

Five-week shop build. Designed around the family's actual pantry inventory — height-adjustable shelves on top, deep pullouts on the bottom, vertical tray storage between.

Materials & details

  • Painted hardwood
  • Inset shaker doors
  • Soft-close hardware
  • Adjustable interior shelving
  • Deep pullout drawer banks
  • Vertical tray dividers
