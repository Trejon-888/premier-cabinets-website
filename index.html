04 — The Process

How it works

Four conversations and a build.

Same shop from first call to final walk-through. We don't sub out the design, the cabinets, or the install.

  1. Step 01

    Consultation

    A site visit or call. We see the room, hear the brief, and tell you whether we're the right shop for it.

  2. Step 02

    Design

    Measured drawings, material selection, hardware specs, and a firm number. No surprises after we start.

  3. Step 03

    Build

    Shop work. Four to ten weeks depending on scope. We update you at each milestone.

  4. Step 04

    Install

    On-site installation by our own crew. We don't sub out the final fit. You walk through, we adjust, you sign off.
