01 — What We Build

Forty-one years of cabinet craft for fine homes.

We build custom kitchens, bathroom cabinetry, paneled rooms, and millwork for homes across Sacramento, the Bay Area, and Northern California. Every piece is made by hand at our Citrus Heights workshop.

From measured drawings to final install, the work passes through fewer hands than at any other shop. The same team that draws the cabinet builds the cabinet and hangs the cabinet.

03 — Our Process

From first meeting to final install.

Four steps. Same shop end to end.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We meet at your home or our Citrus Heights showroom.

  2. 02

    Design

    Measured drawings, material samples, written proposal.

  3. 03

    Build

    Six to ten weeks in the workshop, with weekly photos.

  4. 04

    Install

    One to two weeks on site. Punch list cleared at end.
