01 — What we build

Custom cabinetry and millwork, from our workshop.

We measure, draw, build, and install every piece from our Citrus Heights workshop. Here is what we build, and a sample of how it lives in our clients' homes.

  1. 01.
    Custom Kitchens The whole room measured, drawn, built. Eight to ten weeks in shop, one to two weeks on site.
  2. 02.
    Bathroom Vanities Floating, footed, or integrated. Built around your bathroom's exact layout.
  3. 03.
    Wall Paneling Floor-to-ceiling panels for living rooms, libraries, dining rooms, and entryways.
  4. 04.
    Custom Moldings & Frames Crown, base, casing, and frames cut to match historical or modern profiles.
  5. 05.
    Built-In Bookshelves Library-grade shelving for studies, home offices, and great rooms.
  6. 06.
    Mantels & Fireplace Surrounds Custom-shaped mantels in oak, walnut, or painted hardwood.
  7. 07.
    Restoration & Refinishing Period restoration, cabinet refacing, and full refinishing of existing millwork.

Plus custom millwork tailored to your home.

03 — Our Process

From first meeting to final install.

Four steps. Same shop end to end.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We meet at your home or our Citrus Heights showroom.

  2. 02

    Design

    Measured drawings, material samples, written proposal.

  3. 03

    Build

    Six to ten weeks in the workshop, with weekly photos.

  4. 04

    Install

    One to two weeks on site. Punch list cleared at end.
